All Routes Lead to Collapse

preprint · arXiv, 2026 · sole author · arXiv ↗ · code & data ↗

role & contribution

sole author · independent research

  • derived the reframing identity exposing the flat, norm-blind metric inside softmax routing, with the Boltzmann form obtained from a maximum-entropy principle.
  • measured the concentration signature across 9 pretrained transformers and 4 non-attention routers (graph attention, SSM, recurrent mixer, depth residuals).
  • established causation with two within-model ablations (Mamba selectivity freeze and RWKV carry sweep).

abstract

content-based routing mechanisms — whether multi-head attention, selective state-space scans, or dynamic graph aggregators — systematically concentrate weight onto a tiny subset of anchor positions. we show that attention sinks, representation collapse, and norm stratification are not quirks of attention: they are what content-based routing does under a fixed, norm-blind metric, on any routing axis.
