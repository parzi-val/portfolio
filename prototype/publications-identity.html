Decentralized Digital Identity: A Blockchain and Neural Network Approach

Premier Journal of Science, 2025 · co-author · lead architect (N-SAM)

role & contribution

co-author · lead architect for N-SAM Biometric System

  • architected the N-SAM biometric pipeline for decentralized identity, integrating deep-learning facial embeddings with encrypted off-chain IPFS storage.
  • implemented and benchmarked FaceNet, FaceNet512, and ArcFace across LFW/BUPT datasets, achieving up to 97.65% accuracy and AUC ≈ 0.996.
  • designed the system’s feature extraction, preprocessing, and thresholding workflow.

abstract

digital identity management systems face persistent challenges in privacy, centralized points of failure, and spoofing vulnerabilities. this paper introduces a hybrid architecture combining smart-contract-governed decentralized identity registries with off-chain zero-knowledge neural network biometric verification.
